About FitGauge

FitGauge is a precision-focused fitness and health calculator web application designed to help users clearly understand where they stand across key health metrics.

Instead of offering generic advice or content-heavy experiences, FitGauge treats each metric as a measurement instrument, using clean visuals, accurate formulas, and calm interpretation to provide meaningful context.

Built as a fully client-side, privacy-friendly application with a professional, instrument-style interface, FitGauge emphasizes clarity, accuracy, and trust.
