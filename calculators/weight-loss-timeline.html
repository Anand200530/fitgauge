Daily Calories
Daily Calorie Deficit: 500 calories
A 500-calorie daily deficit leads to approximately 0.5 kg (1.1 lbs) of weight loss per week. This is considered a sustainable and healthy rate for most people.

About Weight Loss Timelines

Weight loss is highly individual. Factors like metabolism, genetics, and adherence affect results. A safe rate is 0.25-1 kg (0.5-2 lbs) per week. Sustainable weight loss typically involves creating a moderate calorie deficit while maintaining adequate protein intake and regular exercise.
