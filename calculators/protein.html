Why protein matters

Protein is essential for building muscle, repairing tissues, and keeping you full. The more active you are, the more protein you need.

Athletes and those trying to build muscle need more protein (1.6-2g per kg of body weight). For fat loss, protein helps preserve muscle while you lose fat.
