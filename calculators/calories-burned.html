📖 How It Works

Calories burned are calculated using the MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) method.

Calories/minute = (MET × 3.5 × weight in kg) / 200
Total Calories = Calories/minute × duration in minutes

MET Values used:

• Walking (3.5 mph): 4 METs | Running (6 mph): 9.8 METs | Cycling (12-14 mph): 8 METs
• Swimming (moderate): 7 METs | Weight Training: 4.5 METs
• Yoga: 2.5 METs | HIIT: 11 METs

* Actual calories burned may vary based on individual factors such as fitness level, intensity, and metabolism.
